Privacy Policy

Princess Dog Walker uses your information only to manage your account, care for your dog, communicate with you, and provide requested services.

Information we collect

When you create an account or make a booking, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, dog details, care instructions, booking information, and messages you choose to provide.

Google sign-in

If you continue with Google, we receive your verified email address and basic profile name from Google. We use this information to create or access your Princess Dog Walker account. We do not receive your Google password.

How information is used

Information is used to provide pet-care services, manage availability and bookings, maintain customer accounts, communicate about requested services, and protect the site from misuse. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and access

Account and booking information is stored in the site’s protected database. Customers can access their own account information, while Julia can access the information needed to operate Princess Dog Walker.

Your choices

You may ask to review, correct, or delete your account information, subject to records that must be retained for legitimate business or legal purposes. You can also stop using Google sign-in and contact Julia about another sign-in option.
